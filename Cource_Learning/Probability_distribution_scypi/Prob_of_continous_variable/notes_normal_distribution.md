### Universal Step-by-Step Approach for Normal Distribution Problems
1. Understand the Question
    Identify:
    => Mean (μ)
    => Standard deviation (σ)
    => What probability range is being asked (above, below, between)?
    => Sample size (if asked about number of people/things).


### Real-World Examples of Normal Distribution
1. Education (Exam Scores) 
    1. Exam marks of a large class often follow a normal distribution.
    2. Helps in grading (e.g., setting cutoffs, percentiles).
2. Manufacturing (Quality Control) 
    1. Size/weight of manufactured products (like bottle filling, screw length).
    2. Used in Six Sigma to detect defects.
3. Finance (Stock Returns)
4. Medicine (Health Metrics)
5. Marketing (Customer Behavior) 
6. Machine Learning
